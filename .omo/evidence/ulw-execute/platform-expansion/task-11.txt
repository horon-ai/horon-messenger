## Decision: implementation path
Chosen: (b) the provider-common sidecar boundary driving a Node sidecar that wraps linejs.
Rejected: (a) a hand-rolled Thrift client. LINE's user-account read/send surface is a Thrift
service whose sessions are normally established with an E2EE handshake, no maintained Rust crate
speaks it, and QA must run against a stub/mock (hard to fake with Thrift binary). The sidecar keeps
this crate pure Rust and lets QA swap in a stub via HYPER_MESSENGER_LINE_SIDECAR.
Recorded in the crate doc comment (crates/providers/line/src/lib.rs lines 1-13).
